Privacy Policy for Mini Genius

Last updated: June 15, 2026

Mini Genius ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in our mobile application.

1. Children's Privacy

Our app is designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. All data processed during gameplay is stored locally on the device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

2. Compliance with Regulations

We take child safety seriously and comply with the following:

  • COPPA: Children's Online Privacy Protection Rule.
  • GDPR-K: General Data Protection Regulation for children.
  • Apple App Store Guideline 1.3: Kids Category requirements.

3. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers. The app tracks game progress (levels unlocked, stars earned, etc.), but this data is stored strictly on the user's device and is never transmitted externally.

4. Advertising

Mini Genius is completely ad-free. We do not use any third-party advertising networks, including Google AdMob or any other contextual or behavioural advertising service. No advertising data is collected, processed, or shared.

Premium features are unlocked exclusively through Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit), which is governed by Apple's own privacy and payment policies.

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses Firebase (by Google) for anonymous crash reporting and basic analytics. These services are configured to:

  • Collect no personally identifiable information (PII).
  • Not use User ID tracking (disabled for COPPA compliance).
  • Collect only anonymous crash stack traces, OS version, and device model.

Firebase Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6. Data Deletion

Because Mini Genius does not use a central server to store accounts, there is no online account to delete. You can delete all local data at any time by going to Settings → Delete Account Data within the app. This permanently wipes all local progress, coins, and settings from your device.
